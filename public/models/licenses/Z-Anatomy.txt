Z-Anatomy — attribution and terms for the reference spinal cord
==============================================================

What is derived from it here
----------------------------

public/models/spinal-cord.glb contains 58 surfaces derived from 35 objects of
the Z-Anatomy Blender scene (Startup.blend): the spinal cord's white matter,
19 white-matter tracts and fasciculi, the anterior and posterior grey horns and
five further grey-matter zones and nuclei, the central canal, the anterior and
posterior roots of the spinal nerves on both sides, the spinal ganglia on both
sides, and the cauda equina.

They are derivatives, not copies. scripts/extract_spinal_cord.py evaluates the
Array, Curve and Mirror modifiers that generate the cord, removes the interior
sagittal septum left where the mirrored halves meet, fills the open ends the
sweep leaves behind, recomputes outward face normals, converts the result from
Blender world metres to RAS millimetres, and translates the whole assembly so
its upper end meets this subject's brainstem. An object whose two mirrored
halves do not touch is published as a left and a right surface; no triangle is
cut. No source vertex is moved relative to any other; the only spatial change
is that one rigid translation.

What ShareAlike means for this bundle
-------------------------------------

CC BY-SA 4.0 is a copyleft licence. Redistributing spinal-cord.glb, or any
adaptation of it, requires attribution as above and requires that the
redistributed material be offered under CC BY-SA 4.0 or a compatible licence.

That obligation attaches to this derived work. The other assets in
public/models/ are derived from different sources under different terms
(FreeSurfer, HCP, NextBrain); see the other files in this directory and
data/sources.json. spinal-cord.glb is a separate file for this reason, and the
manifest records its licence and attribution on its own supplemental layer
entry so a consumer can tell the layers apart.

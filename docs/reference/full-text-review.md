# Full-text review log

Review date: 29 October 2026. Clinical catalogue revision: `2026-10-29`.

This pass checked eight articles against every catalogue association and circuit that cites them. Review covered the complete main prose, including methods, results and discussion, rather than bibliographic metadata alone. Identifiers were compared with the cached reference records. Population, task, laterality, evidence role and atlas approximations were checked; substantive corrections were applied in English and French.

Seven complete article bodies were obtained through NCBI’s PMC full-text service; the eighth was a public copy of the published PDF. Fourteen other PMC requests returned metadata without an article body. Those records retain `abstract` scope. Copies of the articles are not redistributed with the project.

The catalogue now records 43 full-text and 88 abstract extractions among 131 references. Only the eight entries below were newly reviewed in this pass. `full-text` describes the extraction source, not independent specialist approval, numerical reproduction, or verification of every included primary study. Unreviewed image and supplement content is listed explicitly below.

## Statistical and reporting discrepancies retained

Chen’s Table 3 marks cluster-level family-wise-error correction with `*` for p < .05 and `**` for p < .01. The five successive models add praxis, neglect, attention and naming covariates to the baseline. Right fusiform and left lingual are `**` throughout. Right thalamus is `** / ** / ** / unstarred / *`; left thalamus is `unstarred / ** / ** / ** / **`. Right lingual is `unstarred / absent / part of fusiform / absent / part of fusiform`. The catalogue therefore follows the table’s distinctions rather than the narrative’s uniform description of these regions.

Shamay-Tsoory’s methods report 49 patients and 44 healthy controls, whereas the lesion subgroups sum to 50 and Table 2 labels 49 controls. No corrected total is inferred. The overall cognitive group comparison in §3.4 is p = .08; selected follow-up comparisons do not establish a strong anatomical double dissociation. The broad ventromedial definition does not isolate gyrus rectus.

## Remaining scope

The 88 abstract records remain a review backlog. Neither this pass nor the [circuit evidence audit](circuit-evidence.md) establishes a complete causal pathway for every deficit. The catalogue describes group associations and teaching landmarks; clinical accuracy remains unvalidated. This review supports teaching and reference use and does not establish patient-level diagnostic, prognostic or treatment performance.

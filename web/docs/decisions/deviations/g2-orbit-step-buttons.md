# Orbit step buttons in the View popover

Chunk: G2
Status: approved by the user on #4 (2026-10-25)

## Legacy behaviour

The camera orbits by pointer drag or by arrows on the canvas (render/keyboard-orbit.js); there is no single-pointer control.

## New behaviour

Step buttons (left, right, up, down) in the View popover orbit by the keyboard step (ORBIT_STEP). Added after parity.

## Standard cited

WCAG 2.2 SC 2.5.7 Dragging Movements.
